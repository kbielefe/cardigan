# Domain sources

Saved 2026-10-17 for the LK150 and TH-160 reading. These are the publisher's own pages or the manuals Knit It Now hosted. The live Knit It Now PDF links returned 404, so the two Taitexma manuals are the 2024 Internet Archive copies. City Farmhouse Studio blocked a direct download, so those patterns are archive copies of the free pages. Ad-free Etsy PDFs were left alone.

## Manuals

- `manuals/Silver Reed LK-150 Knitting Machine.pdf`. Silver Reed LK150 instruction book, 55 pages, added 2026-10-17. It is a scan (pdfFactory, 2011) and the pages do not yield selectable text.
- `manuals/taitexma-th-160-instruction.pdf`. Taitexma TH-160 / 230 instruction manual, 49 pages, scanned 2021-05-06, archived 2024-06-17 from `knititnow.com/MachineDocsPDF/160main.pdf`. The text names the change lever stops as Intarsia (1), Plain (P1), and Part (P2). It includes plain knitting, intarsia, and tuck.
- `manuals/taitexma-tr-160-230-instruction.pdf`. Taitexma TR-160/230 ribber manual, 43 pages, same scan and archive, from `160ribber.pdf`. The text has steps for 1×1 rib, full needle rib, circular knitting, and English rib, plus racking. Fisherman rib is not in this text.

## Patterns

- `patterns/eileen-montgomery-lk150-fairisle.pdf`. Eileen Montgomery, LK150 Fairisle, © 1999, 2008, from her freebies page. Four fairisle methods, including method 4 and the FC6.
- `patterns/clearwater-lk150-fairisle-method-1.html`, `method-2`, `method-3`. Irene Woods, Clearwater Knits, archived 2011-05-18. Method 1 is the LK150 manual's slip fairisle. Method 4 was not in that archive.
- `patterns/city-farmhouse-wrap-sweater-scarf.html`. City Farmhouse Studio, 2025-01-23, archived 2025-10-13. Three sizes, tuck, hand-transfer rib.
- `patterns/city-farmhouse-simple-stitch-sweater.html`. 2025-09-26, archived 2025-10-13. Nine sizes, short-row neck.
- `patterns/city-farmhouse-summer-in-the-city.html`. 2022, archived 2024-11-26. The `90 (98, 106, 114) 122, 138 [146, 154, 170]` size line.
- `patterns/city-farmhouse-summer-breeze.html`. Archived 2022-10-20, an early copy of the free page.
- `patterns/creative-tien-lk150-tuck-blanket.html`. Creative Tien, 2022-05-05, archived 2025-11-18. Russell levers and D position. Parts 2 and 3 are videos, not pages.

## References

- `references/clearwater-abbreviations.html`. Clearwater Knits machine-knitting abbreviation list, saved from the live site.
- `references/clearwater-stitch-symbols.html`. The same site's stitch-symbol scan, which it says is from the LK150 manual.

## Not in this folder

- The household wheelchair blanket, `example_pattern.html`. It is not in this tree.
